## Feature A — Packing Workflow Hero

Replaces the former standalone box / Dashboard mock with a custom self-contained SVG at `public/assets/packing-workstation.svg`.

- E-commerce packing worker puts an item in a cardboard parcel
- A camera mounted overhead records the packing workbench
- A barcode scanner reads an order's shipping label
- PACKTOOK screen displays order barcode, camera preview and REC status
- Three annotations: SCAN BARCODE / PACKTOOK REC / SCAN AGAIN → SAVE
- Accessible, descriptive `img` alt and a disclaimer stating that this is a conceptual illustration, not a customer recording
- Follows PACKTOOK Customer CI (Navy, Blue and Cyan)

**Important**: This is a VECTOR ILLUSTRATION of the workflow, not a real packing photograph. It can be replaced with an approved real photo later without changing the section markup.

## Feature B — Plan Comparison Table

Replaces six unconnected pricing cards with one responsive HTML comparison table.

- Columns: Free / Starter / Basic / Growth / Business / Pro
- Rows (9): video quota, device limit, subscription period, Barcode start-stop, local recording, dashboard history, Help/Troubleshooting, target business, prelaunch availability
- Prices/test data from `production/payment-functions/index.js`: Free 0, Starter 199, Basic 299, Growth 599, Business 1290, Pro 1990 THB
- Video credits: Free 20/month, Starter 50, Basic 300, Growth 1,500, Business 5,000, Pro unlimited (during paid entitlement)
- Device limits: 1 / 1 / 1 / 3 / 5 / 10
- Desktop: all six plans side-by-side; mobile: horizontal scroll with left-hand row header sticky, `tabindex="0"` for keyboard access
- Free and paid plans display **Prelaunch / No checkout**. TEST pricing is marked as preliminary.
- Nothing in the new section can call a payment endpoint.
