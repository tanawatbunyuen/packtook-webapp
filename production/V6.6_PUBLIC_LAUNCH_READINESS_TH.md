## Launch strategy

Launch in two independent phases:

1. **Phase A — Public Free:** verified email, legal consent, backend-provisioned Free 20 videos per month and 1 registered device. No real payments or paid upgrades.
2. **Phase B — Paid subscriptions:** only after separate Omise production credentials/security audit, webhook/reconciliation, invoices/tax/refund/subscription terms, actual payment and monitoring QA. **V6.6 does not enable Phase B.**

Never treat previous virtual-clock or Omise Test Mode PASS as proof of live billing.

## Required before Phase A: public Free

### 1. Legal and support (owner action)

The current policies at `app/policy-terms.html` and `app/policy-privacy.html` are explicitly DRAFT.
Before launch, the owner/legal reviewer must supply and approve:
- Correct registered service provider (individual or entity), support and privacy contact channel
- Effective date and current policy version; a clear means for customers to exercise privacy rights and delete account data
- Categories/purposes/retention of account, device, order metadata and billing data; video remains local and needs user backups
- Free usage rules (20 videos/month, one device), credit reset semantics, fair use, cancellation and service limitations
- Published contact/support pages and internal process for support, security incidents and deletion requests
Do not place invented addresses or contact email in the policies.

### 2. Firebase/Auth/App Check (controlled QA)

- Confirm Customer Auth email/password provider enabled, authorized domains, verification and password-reset email templates.
- Configure reCAPTCHA Enterprise for the **Customer domain**, and set its PUBLIC site key in `app/signup-config.js`. No secret/server key goes into HTML.
- Verify backend callable `completeFreeSignup` enforces App Check and checks live Auth email verification, consent policy version and account claim.
- Verify `publicSignupStatus` is deployed on target region and returns disabled before gates are authorized.
- **Important:** `production/functions/index.js` deliberately locks its Firebase project to `packtook-prod-01`. Do not simply deploy it to `packtook-staging` and assume QA will work. A separately reviewed Emulator/QA project adaptation is required for fully isolated integration testing.
- Do not enable all three backend flags simply to test on a live customer database.

### 3. End-to-end acceptance (isolated QA)

1. Registration link with server flags OFF → login stays Beta / no Free grant.
2. Wrong or missing App Check → completeFreeSignup denied.
3. New email registration → verification email arrives.
4. Unverified user cannot open Free, record or obtain credits.
5. Verified user consents to approved Terms/Privacy → one Free account created in Firestore, claim applied and token refreshed.
6. Free account has 20 videos remaining, 1 device; second device blocked.
7. Scan barcode → record → scan same code → .webm file saved locally and Dashboard searchable → 19 Free remaining.
8. Re-login / retry activation → still 19, no repeat Free credit grant, no paid-plan overwrite.
9. Payment QR not accessible to public Free (Omise TEST remains tester-only).
10. Error during claim update can retry safely; deletion/abuse cannot renew grant.
11. Beta accounts and their recordings remain normal; no function regression.
12. Disable Public access and validate fresh backend calls rejected for public while Beta remains authorized.

Capture timestamped screenshots, Function logs (redacted), Auth/Firestore state in QA and signoff. Never use real customer data/UIDs as fixtures.

### 4. Public customer experience and commercial disclosure

- Marketing currently shows TEST prices for six plans and says prelaunch. Before Phase A, approve the text or explicitly hide paid price offers; do not advertise payments as open.
- Publish approved policies with effective date.
- Update static Beta-only labels and turn the hidden Free CTA into the public signup entry **only after the backend is ready**.
- Keep Omise Live off for Free-first rollout; do not convert 50/300/etc test credits into a paid promise.
- Remove marketing noindex/robots restrictions only when the owner explicitly approves SEO and the policies/contact pages are available.

### 5. Operations / cost / rollback

- Setup Cloud Functions logs/alerts, Firebase Auth signup abuse controls, budget alerts, quota monitoring and a support incident process.
- Document exact rollback: server-side access flag OFF and redeploy required Functions configuration (flag changes are not automatically instant when stored as deployed environment variables), then verify public calls are denied. Do not touch Production Beta claims, video files or wallets.
- Revert Marketing/Customer Hosting separately if UI is broken.
- Owner signoff needed before any public DNS/Marketing announcement.
