## Separate IAM and runtime permissions (not approved)

The intended runtime needs carefully reviewed service-scoped permissions for:
- HMAC Secret access on the individual \`PACKTOOK_PUBLIC_FREE_EMAIL_HMAC_KEY_HEX\` Secret.
- Firebase Auth get user / update custom claims.
- Firestore read/write transactions for account, guard and pilot controls.
- Deployment operator permission to attach the new Service Account (\`roles/iam.serviceAccountUser\` to the deployer on that account), rather than granting that role to the runtime.

Do not grant blanket Editor/Owner roles or project-wide Secret Accessor; do not assume Firestore Rules protect Admin SDK operations. IAM changes must be separately designed and approved after account creation.
