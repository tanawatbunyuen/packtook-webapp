# PACKTOOK V6.2.1 — Owner Revenue & Expiry Report

**Project:** `packtook-prod-01` (Production only)
**Branch:** `feature/v6.2.1-owner-revenue-expiry`
**Status:** Source committed and mocked regression tests passed. Not deployed by the assistant. Customer V6.1.2 stays untouched.

## Why the two issues appeared

1. The V6.2 Owner dashboard intentionally excluded the revenue section because Omise Live is not enabled in Production. V6.2.1 restores a **read-only financial ledger report**, but does not activate payments.
2. The Production Customer Free plan has **no expiration date**. V6.2 rendered null expiration as `—`; V6.2.1 now says **ไม่มีกำหนด (Free)**. Paid plans with an ISO `accounts/{uid}.planExpiresAt` show date + days remaining; paid plans with no expiry show **ยังไม่กำหนดวันหมดอายุ**. The UI does not invent an expiry.

## Revenue reporting logic

- Firebase callable: `ownerAnalyticsRevenue` in dedicated `owner` codebase, region `asia-southeast1`.
- Protected by Production-only custom claim `packtookPlatformOwner`, verified email and current Firebase Auth Owner record. Not accessible to ordinary Customer accounts.
- Scans Production `accounts/{uid}/billingRequests` documents with `status=paid` (up to 100 per account and up to 1200 accounts). A partial-data warning appears when truncated. This operation reads Firestore and may incur reads/billing; it does not create a charge or change account balances.
- **Live**: paid record with `paymentProvider=omise`, `omiseLivemode=true`, and an apparently live charge ID.
- **Test**: paid record from Omise with `chrg_test_` charge ID, provided mode is not explicitly true. Supports older test records without the mode flag.
- **Unclassified**: missing/conflicting mode metadata or invalid amount. Never silently add to Live revenue.
- Metrics show cumulative values; monthly table covers recent 12 months; by-plan totals use the package on each billing record, not the customer's current plan.
- These are Firestore **paid records**, NOT verified bank deposits, net revenue after refunds, or real-time Omise settlement. The backend still has no payment-creating API.
- With no paid transactions in Production, all recorded-sales amounts correctly show `฿0` and the banner explains **ยังไม่เปิดรับชำระเงินจริง**. Failed data loads show **—** and an error rather than fictitious zeroes.
