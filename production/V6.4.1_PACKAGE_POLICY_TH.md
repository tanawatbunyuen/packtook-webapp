# PACKTOOK V6.4.1 — Package Purchase Policy (10-day Credit Grace + No Downgrade)

**Source branch:** `feature/v6.4.1-package-policy` (based on V6.4 Payment Hardening)
**Firebase target:** `packtook-prod-01` (explicitly guarded)
**Status:** implemented in GitHub; tested in mocked Firebase/Omise, NOT deployed by assistant.
**Payment mode:** Omise **TEST only**; no Credit Card or real-money receiving.

## Business rules

1. **Paid package expires**: recording entitlement stops **immediately** at `planExpiresAt`; existing finite video credits are retained (not usable for recording) up to `planExpiresAt + 10 * 24 hours` to be carried into the next purchase.
2. **At exact ten-day boundary**: carryover remains eligible up to and including the exact `planExpiresAt + 10 days`. At any moment after that timestamp the old credit is worth **zero for carryover**, irrespective of whether the physical cleanup job ran.
3. **Daily reset**: `promptpayExpireUnusedCredits` scheduled function (03:15 Asia/Bangkok) physically sets expired finite `creditWalletRemaining` to 0 and writes an audit log. Bounded scan of 800 accounts/day, Firestore transaction, skips active/re-purchased accounts and in-flight credit reservations. Accounts past this limit or temporarily skipped are caught on a later run. Thus accounting forfeiture is effective at the precise deadline; stored cleanup occurs at the next successful sweep.
4. **Downgrade**: No purchase of a lower-priced/lower-tier package, whether the current plan is Active, within 10-day expiry grace or expired longer. E.g. Pro → Business always forbidden, Basic → Starter always forbidden even after expiry.
5. **Upgrade with valid existing credits**: Old remaining credits are added to newly purchased credits **inside the Omise-verified Firestore payment transaction**. Example Starter 120 → Basic 300 = **420** available after Paid.
6. **Expired during grace**: Same-tier renewal or upgrade allowed with full eligible credit carryover. E.g. Starter with 45 credits and expiry 9 days ago → Basic = **345** credits.
7. **Expired beyond 10 days**: Same tier/higher can still be purchased but with **zero** old credit. E.g. Starter 45 credits and expiry 11 days ago → Basic = **300** credits.
8. **No reset while pending**: Unpaid QR request does not change plan/credit. Existing reservations block buying, and the scheduled reset skips accounts with outstanding reserved credits.
9. **Free plan**: Existing monthly free quota is unchanged. Initial Free→paid conversion still transfers the remaining Free credits. Pro's unlimited plan remains unlimited; Pro cannot downgrade to a finite plan.
10. **Expiry after purchase**: The existing rule stays: active paid purchases add 30 days from the current expiration, while expired renewals/upgrades start a new 30-day period from verified payment time.

## Code changes

- `payment-functions/index.js`: single authoritative `purchaseDecision`, `carryoverCredits`, `purchasedCreditBalance`, `retainedCredits`, scheduled `promptpayExpireUnusedCredits`; amount and identity still verified against Omise Test Charge before plan/credit changes.
- `app/app.js`: downgrade buttons disabled even on expired plans; Customer plan/credit summary distinguishes **usable 0** during expiry from **retained credits** for renewal.
- `owner-functions/index.js` and `owner/app.js`: Owner customer directory displays `retained` balance and retention deadline; no credit grants to expired wallets and no new mutation permissions.
- `scripts/test-package-policy.mjs`: exact 10-day boundary, no downgrade, active upgrade credit math, expired renewal, legacy paid and Free rules.
- `scripts/test-promptpay.mjs`: simulated Omise QR Paid for Starter→Basic with 120+300 credits, 9-day expiry credit rollover, 11-day expiry no rollover, and daily scheduled credit reset with audit/no double reset.
- Guarded deploy scripts and static verification updated to require policy tests before deploy.

## Isolation and rollback

`functions:beta`, Firestore Rules, Marketing and Staging are unchanged in this branch. Only Payment Functions, Owner Functions, Customer Hosting and Owner Hosting require deploy.

If your Production Test suite reports unexpected results, stop before deployment. For a rollback, redeploy the V6.4 branch using the same explicitly guarded scripts. **Important**: Rolling back scheduled functions may cause the Firebase CLI to offer deleting the new scheduled function — review the prompt rather than accepting unintended deletion. Account credits already forfeited with audit are not automatically restored by rolling back code.

Expiry forfeiture is irreversible without an explicitly authorized manual compensation flow. Start with a dedicated Production Beta QR Tester account (no real customer orders) and inspect all scheduled-job logs before broad rollout.
