# PACKTOOK V6.4.2 — Same-tier Renewal + 7-day In-app Expiry Reminder

**Branch:** `feature/v6.4.2-same-plan-renewal-reminders`  
**Base:** V6.4.1 Package Policy  
**Target Firebase:** `packtook-prod-01`  
**Status:** code pushed to GitHub, mock regression tested. **NOT deployed**.  
**Payment:** Omise **TEST QR only**, no live money, no credit card.

## Business rules

1. **Same-tier repurchase any time**: A Starter customer can purchase Starter again **even with 21 days left or 120 unused credits**; Basic→Basic, Growth→Growth, Business→Business and Pro→Pro follow the same rule.
2. **7-day advance notice**: From exactly 7×24 hours before the current paid plan expires, PACKTOOK Customer App displays a visible banner on all authenticated pages: days remaining, local expiry time (Asia/Bangkok) and a **ต่ออายุแพ็กเกจ** button to Packages. The warning stays visible until expiration or payment renewal, and updates on account refresh, tab visibility and once per hour while the page stays open. A second warning appears on Packages and Users.
3. **7 days is NOT a purchase restriction.** Renewal remains available even more than 7 days before expiry.
4. **Credit carryover**: All eligible unused finite wallet credits **plus the purchased allowance** after verified Omise Paid. Example active Starter with 120 left, re-buy Starter 50 → **170 credits**. No reset on request or pending QR.
5. **Expiry extension**: A paid renewal while the account is still active extends the old expiration by 30 days. After expiry, a successful renewal begins 30 days from verified payment time.
6. **Post-expiry rule preserved**: For 10×24 hours after expiry, unused credits can be carried into same/higher tier. Beyond 10 days, no old credits transfer and the existing daily forfeit job continues.
7. **Downgrade forbidden**: A lower tier is unavailable even when current paid plan has expired; existing reserve-credit blocking and Open Billing Request protection remain.
8. **No duplicate credit**: Signed Omise Test verification, Firestore billing version, immutable request and transaction idempotency remain unchanged.

## Notification delivery boundary

**This version implements an in-app reminder only.** It is shown when the customer opens or keeps the PACKTOOK website signed in. It does **not** email, SMS, send a browser push notification, or notify a customer who never opens the app. Those require a separately authorized communications workflow.

## Important limitations / rollout safety

Omise credentials remain `skey_test_` and test webhook secret; **no real payment or card checkout**. The daily expired-credit cleanup job from V6.4.1 could affect existing overdue Production test wallets when deployed; inspect account data and scheduled-job logs before deploying. Use dedicated Production Beta QR Tester accounts rather than customer accounts operating live packing tasks.

Rollback can redeploy the guarded Customer/Payment scripts from V6.4.1, but this would restore the previous restriction that same-tier renewals needed exhausted credits or <=7 days remaining. Any credits already settled by a Test payment remain in Firestore unless separately reconciled by an authorized operator.
